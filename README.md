# Curriculum for the USB Film School

A 64GB USB drive can hold about 60 hours of video content at a reasonable home viewing quality, and it costs under $5 in the year of our lord 2025.

This is about 30 films.

Online streaming services have thousands of films, but most of them are mediocre and plenty of them are downright
terrible.  "Recommendations" by The Algorithm reinforce but don't enlighten.

The goal is to collect 30-50 quality films around a particular theme, such that if anyone interested in movies (which is many people to some degree) were to be given a USB drive containing them, they might be curious and plug it in, and find a collection of immediately watchable movies.  Some of which they've seen before and liked, many they've heard of and have positive impressions about, and a few they've never heard of.

Watching any of the films on its own is enriching/rewarding; watching the whole set is an amateur film education.

## Themes

Themes should be instantly graspable and compelling to a general audience, while allowing for a rich selection of important films. 
Accessible hooks and deep enough to support serious film study. Each includes both crowd-pleasers and more challenging works while maintaining thematic coherence.

- Mind Games
- Conmen and Heists

## Rubric for selection

- +8 for being on several "greatest films ever made" lists
- +4 underrepresented genre within the theme
- +1 for a really famous person being in it
  - +1 if it's early in their career
- -2 if the director has other movies in the theme
